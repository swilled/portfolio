desktop trading terminal · futures · equities · crypto

Every tick.Zero lag.

Speed is the charting platform I built for my own trading after TradingView, Quantower, Deepcharts, MotiveWave and NinjaTrader all fell short. One app for charts, orderflow and execution, built around one rule: the screen never falls behind the market.

watch the 60s demo how it's built

depth of market · dom

The book, live.

Every level of the order book updates in place at full frame rate, so you can watch liquidity move without the ladder jumping around.

  • Level-2 depth over a 24-byte binary protocol
  • Order ticket with bracket targets and stops
  • Kill switch, flatten and cancel-all always in reach

order flow · footprint

Bid × ask, every price.

Footprint bars split each candle into the volume that traded on the bid and on the ask at every price, with imbalances lit up so aggressive buying and selling stand out.

time & sales

Every print, as it lands.

The tape streams every trade the moment it lands, colored by side, so you can feel the pace of the market and not just see it.

volume profile

Where size actually traded.

Volume profiles show the prices where the most size changed hands, built from the tick stream rather than minute bars.

replay

The strategy, replayed.

Replay a session tick by tick with your strategy running against it, and watch the equity curve, win rate and drawdown build as it trades.

architecture

Three layers, one direction.

Market data enters once and flows down through three layers, each handing the next a zero-copy view of the same memory.

01 · native host

Rust · Tauri v2 · mimalloc

  • 1ms multimedia timer
  • elevated ingestion thread
  • shared-memory IPC with FNV-1a checks
lock-free ring buffer
02 · market data engine

TypeScript · WebAssembly · Workers

  • 24-byte binary wire protocol
  • vectorized EMA, VWAP, bands
  • LTTB downsampler
offscreen canvas transfer
03 · rendering pipeline

Canvas 2D · WebGL

  • 3-tile sliding viewport
  • glyph atlas + precompiled paths
  • double-buffered swap chains
