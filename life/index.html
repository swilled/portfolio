Alpine

What pulls me up there is the places. Glacier water so blue it looks fake, goats that couldn't care less that you showed up, a ridge turning pink at sunrise while the valley is still asleep. It's quiet up there in a way nothing else is, and everything gets simple: keep moving, watch the weather, look out for whoever's with you.

Alpinism is how I get more of that. Every trip asks a little more of me, and the ones that turn me around, like Adams, teach me the most. The long game is to become a good enough mountaineer for the hardest peaks in the world, Denali first.
